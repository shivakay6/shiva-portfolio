### Case Study 2: Smart Reviewer Discovery — AI-Powered Reviewer Recommendations
**Company:** Kriyadocs · **Role:** Product Manager
**Tags:** Generative AI · LLMs · Semantic Matching · Decision Support

**One-line summary (card only):** Used LLM-based semantic matching to pair manuscripts with the right peer reviewers — and generative AI to explain why each reviewer fits — so editors find reviewers faster while staying in control.

**Card headline results:** 40% less time to identify reviewers · 25% faster Time to First Decision · AI-generated rationale for every recommendation

**Problem:**
Academic editors spend considerable time identifying suitable peer reviewers for submitted manuscripts. Finding researchers with relevant subject-matter expertise often requires manual searches and assessment of research publications, creating delays in the peer review process. The opportunity was to use AI to accelerate reviewer discovery while keeping editors in control of final reviewer selection.

**What I did:**
- **Identified the opportunity:** Focused on reviewer discovery as a high-friction step in the editorial workflow where AI could reduce manual effort.
- **Defined the solution approach:** Worked on an AI-powered Reviewer Recommendation Engine using LLM-based semantic analysis to match manuscript content with potential reviewers' expertise — matching on meaning and research context rather than just keywords.
- **Added explainability with generative AI:** Used an LLM to generate a clear, plain-language explanation of why each recommended reviewer is suited to that particular manuscript, so editors can trust and verify recommendations quickly.
- **Translated the problem into product requirements:** Focused the feature on surfacing relevant candidates and improving the efficiency of reviewer discovery.
- **Designed for editorial decision-making:** Positioned AI recommendations as decision support, allowing editors to assess and select reviewers rather than automating the final decision.
- **Integrated the capability into the workflow:** Worked with the team to bring reviewer recommendations into the peer review experience.

**Results:**
- Reduced reviewer identification time by 40%.
- Reduced Time to First Decision by 25%.
- Streamlined reviewer discovery through LLM-based semantic matching between manuscript content and reviewer expertise.
- Gave editors a clear, generative AI rationale for every recommended reviewer.
- Helped editors spend less time searching for candidates and more time evaluating reviewer suitability.

### Case Study 4: Third-Party Integrations — Connecting the Publishing Ecosystem
**Company:** Kriyadocs · **Role:** Product Manager
**Tags:** Integrations · APIs · Platform

**One-line summary (card only):** Brought plagiarism screening, copyright payments and image integrity checks inside the platform instead of separate tools.

**Card headline results:** 3 specialist services integrated · Less workflow fragmentation · Connected end-to-end publishing experience

**Problem:**
Academic publishing workflows depend on specialized external services for plagiarism screening, copyright-related payments and image integrity checks. When these capabilities operate separately from the core publishing platform, users face fragmented workflows, additional steps and operational dependencies. The opportunity was to integrate third-party services into Kriyadocs so users could access these capabilities as part of a more connected publishing experience.

**What I did:**
- **Identified integration opportunities:** Worked on incorporating external services into Kriyadocs to support essential publishing workflows without requiring users to manage disconnected processes.
- **Defined integration requirements:** Translated workflow needs into product requirements covering how users initiate requests, submit relevant content, access results or transaction statuses, and continue their publishing tasks.
- **Designed end-to-end workflows:** Considered how external services fit into the existing product experience, including interactions between Kriyadocs and third-party systems.
- **Managed cross-functional dependencies:** Collaborated with engineering and relevant stakeholders to align integration requirements, technical constraints and expected behavior.
- **Considered exceptions and reliability:** Addressed workflow considerations around service responses, failed requests, status visibility and handling integration outcomes.
- **Maintained a cohesive product experience:** Focused on making third-party capabilities part of the broader publishing workflow rather than isolated tools.

**Integrations:**
- **iThenticate:** Integrated plagiarism screening and similarity-checking capabilities for scholarly manuscripts.
- **CCC (Copyright Clearance Center):** Integrated payment workflows associated with copyright permissions within the publishing process.
- **ImageTwin:** Integrated image integrity checks to help identify potential image manipulation or duplication concerns in scholarly content.

**Results (heading on page: "Product impact"):**
- Extended Kriyadocs' capabilities through integrations with specialist publishing services.
- Reduced workflow fragmentation by making external capabilities accessible within the broader publishing process.
- Supported a more connected publishing experience across submission, peer review, production and delivery.
