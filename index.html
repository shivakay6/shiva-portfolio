About

I'm a Product Manager with 5+ years of product experience, including 3+ years building and scaling enterprise B2B SaaS products. Today I'm building Midas at ProManage.ai — an AI-powered Conversation-to-Conversion platform that turns customer conversations into business outcomes.

Before that, I was the founding PM for Kriyadocs' enterprise Peer Review platform, taking it from 0 to 1 and owning it across the full product lifecycle — through 40+ releases and past $1M in ARR.

My work spans discovery, strategy, roadmapping, AI-powered capabilities, analytics, integrations and go-to-market. I enjoy working close to customers, understanding complex problems, and turning them into simple, scalable product experiences. I use rapid, AI-assisted prototyping to validate ideas with customers and align teams before engineering commits.

I came to product through engineering, B2B solution consulting and technical sales, followed by an MBA from TAPMI Manipal. That path shapes how I make decisions: balancing what customers need, what technology can do, and what the business needs to win.

I'm most excited by B2B SaaS and AI products that solve meaningful business problems and create measurable customer value.
